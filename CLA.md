Rudy is AGPL-3.0-or-later. That license is what everyone receives the code
under, and nothing here changes it or takes it away from anyone.

This agreement exists for one reason: the project is also licensed
commercially, to people who cannot take AGPL terms. Selling that exception
means the copyright holder has to hold the rights to every line in it, and a
contribution nobody granted those rights to cannot be in it. So the choice is
this agreement, or a contribution that has to be rewritten before it can ship
under anything but the AGPL.

signing your work away.
